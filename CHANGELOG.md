# Changelog

Studio-facing notes for each release. Add a line under "Unreleased" in every pull request.
When a batch goes out, move the lines under a new version heading (`vYYYY.MM.N`) and publish a
GitHub Release with the same text. See `docs/release-process.md`.

## Unreleased

- Attendance and make-up credits now save for real studios. On Today, tap Attended, or "Absent or cancelled…" to record a no-show, a studio cancellation, a family cancellation with notice or a late family cancellation; "Mark all attended" does the whole day, and the page shows how many lessons are not marked. A studio cancellation always gives a make-up credit; a family cancellation with notice gives one unless you untick it; a late cancellation or a no-show gives one only if you tick "Give a make-up anyway". The form reminds you of your studio's cancel notice and make-up allowance, and how many credits the student had in the last 90 days. The calendar's "Cancel this week" now asks who cancelled. Credits last 90 days for now (studios have no term dates yet) and can be extended by 30 days. On the student profile you see the credits, book a make-up into a free time (it goes on the calendar marked Make-up and the family gets a chat message), remove a make-up to give the credit back, and the last 12 weeks of attendance. Families see credits owed with a use-by date, can press Request a make-up, and see recent lessons as Attended, Cancelled or Missed. Studio Snapshot's Lessons delivered counts real marks. Marking never changes an invoice and never adds a late fee; Bill next term and renewals stay hidden. Needs migration 0025_attendance_makeups (apply before merging).
- Real studios no longer see demo-only features. Hidden on real studios (the demo studio keeps them): Bill next term and Renewal invitations & reminders on Invoices (they still run on the demo timetable; an old link shows "not available for your studio yet"), Tax Lite and Pro CFO on Snapshot (they still read demo figures), the Video lesson policy and "Sent notifications (simulated)" in Settings, the canned "Show translation" in chat, and make-up credits in the family app. Families only see practice tasks once the studio has set some (no more empty "No assignments yet"), and the family tour skips the practice-tasks card on real studios. No migration.
- Students → Enrol now saves every step for real studios. The welcome lesson ticks or ratings, starting stages, course template, grade hours, lesson minutes, target term, practice goal, strengths and what we'll work on are saved on Continue, Back and Finish later, so the wizard reopens on the step you left, on any device. Before, a refresh or Finish later sent the student back to step 1 with everything lost. If a save fails you stay on the step and see why. Target-term choices now start from the current term (they were fixed at Term 2 2027 onwards), in Enrol and in Library → Assign to students, and activation stamps today's date instead of the demo date. The first-week assignment list is still demo-only; real families get its text in the welcome summary in chat. Needs migration 0024_enrolment_progress (until it is run, the wizard keeps progress for the session only, as before).
- Practice minutes and lesson notes now save for real studios. Parents (and students on the family app) log practice from Progress and the teacher sees it straight away on the student profile's weekly chart, which is now shown for real studios too. On Today, each lesson has Add note; a note with "Share with family" turned on appears in the family app, and private notes never leave the studio. Studio Snapshot lists a student nobody has logged practice for yet as "No practice logged yet" instead of counting them as at risk. Parents can log practice for today or up to 30 days back,. Nobody can delete a practice entry from the app yet. Attendance and make-up credits are still not saved for real studios. Needs migration 0023_practice_and_notes (apply before merging).
- Families of real studios now see more than invoices and chat. Home shows their child's next lesson and the next 4 weeks from the studio calendar, with cancelled weeks crossed out and moved lessons marked. Studio news meant for them appears on Home and in News (whole studio, their family, or a group their child is in). Progress shows their child's real course: goal, a bar per area and piece stages, with only the feedback the teacher marked as shared. Before, these screens read demo data and were empty for real studios. The server sends parents a family-safe copy, so private notes and other families never reach their phone. News photos are not shown to families yet. No migration.
- Students get their own guided tour. The first time someone opens the family app, the tour asks "I'm the student" or "I'm a parent or guardian". Students are shown their next lesson, practice tasks, progress and their teacher's marking, how to log practice, studio news, chatting with their teacher, help and languages, in plain words and without invoices. Parents get the same walk-through plus invoices and email alerts. Both are in English, 中文 and Tiếng Việt, can be skipped, and replay from the ? button (which asks again, so a child and parent can each take theirs on the same phone). Family chat now shows the studio's teacher name instead of always "Ms Clarke". No migration.
- Studio-to-family onboarding fixes (22 Nov 2026 audit). Parents who sign in to a real studio now see their own studio in the family portal. Before, parents can't read the studio record under the database rules, so the app quietly fell back to the demo studio's families, invoices and chat; the studio now comes from the parent's own record on the server, and if it can't load the parent gets "Your studio didn't load" instead of demo data. Teachers can't send a family invite until the studio terms are published (families used to get a link that said the terms weren't published yet). Sending the invite again after a failed email no longer adds the same child twice. A parent whose access was revoked can't get it back by filling in a new invite link; the owner restores it on the student's profile. Finding an existing owner or team member by email now looks past the first 200 accounts. A real studio with no RESEND_API_KEY now shows the email error instead of saying "sent". Future birth years are refused on invite. No migration.
- Choosing 中文 or VI now translates the whole signed-in app, not just the menu: Today, Students, enrolment, import, groups, Calendar, Chat, News, Invoices (including Bill next term and renewals), Settings, studio terms settings, the Library and courses, Snapshot, Tax Lite and Pro CFO, the studio tour and setup guide, sign-in and two-step sign-in. Dates and times are shown the local way (11月16日 周一, T2, 16 thg 11, 15:30). Messages, news posts and notes people write are never translated. Still English on purpose: studio agreement clause wording, consent statements in Vietnamese, the reviewed GST/ATO tax wording, the studio help articles and the Keystone admin console (see `docs/i18n.md`). No migration.
- Clearer sign-in link errors. When an emailed sign-in link does not work, the page now says why: the link expired or a newer one was sent (only the newest link works), access was revoked, too many attempts, or a connection problem, instead of always saying "expired or already used". The "Check your inbox" page says only the newest link works. When Keystone creates a studio for someone who already has a Keystone account, they now also get a sign-in link email (before, only brand-new owners got one, from Supabase), and a revoked email can no longer be made a studio owner until access is restored. No migration.
- Xero sync for Pro studios. The studio owner presses Connect Xero in Settings → Integrations, signs in to Xero and allows Keystone, then picks the income account for lessons and the bank account families pay into. From then on every sent invoice (never drafts), the family as a Xero contact, and every payment go to Xero by themselves every 15 minutes, or straight away with Sync now. GST-registered studios get GST on Income lines. Credit notes, voids and changes after sending are not sent yet. Xero tokens are stored encrypted and never reach the browser. Needs migration 0022_xero_sync, the XERO_CLIENT_ID and XERO_CLIENT_SECRET settings and a 15-minute job (steps in docs/security/xero.md). Keystone stops at 5 connected studios, Xero's free limit.
- Families can pay invoices online with Stripe. A studio owner presses Connect with Stripe in Settings → Integrations, sets up (or links) their own Stripe account on Stripe's own pages, and comes back connected. Families then see Pay now on their invoices and pay on a secure Stripe page; the money goes straight to the studio's Stripe account (Stripe charges the studio its standard fees, Keystone takes nothing). The payment shows on the invoice by itself a few seconds later, and the invoice turns paid or part paid. Card, plus BECS direct debit if the studio turns it on in Stripe. Until a studio connects, families see bank details as before. The Xero button for real studios now says "coming soon" instead of a pretend connection. Needs migration 0021_stripe_connect and the STRIPE_CONNECT_WEBHOOK_SECRET setting (steps in docs/security/stripe-connect.md).
- Works better on phones and tablets. The sign-in page no longer shows a language switcher (languages are on the public website and inside the app after you sign in). In the studio app on a phone, the header is one short row with a Menu button that opens language, larger text, help, your plan and sign out, instead of filling half the screen. The family portal no longer repeats the logo and studio name under the app header, and its bottom tabs fit on small phones. The left menu on tablets scrolls when the screen is short (iPad landscape). The six authenticator code boxes fit on small phones (they ran off the right edge), and long email addresses and course stage pickers no longer widen the page. No migration.
- Studio terms setup is easier. The studio address now suggests Australian addresses and suburbs as you type, and picking one also fills in your State (needs the GOOGLE_PLACES_API_KEY setting; without it the box works as before). The separate "Privacy contact email" question is gone: privacy requests go to your studio email unless you set a different one in Settings. The public holiday question now has clear choices (no lesson and no charge, no lesson with a make-up, or lessons go ahead as normal) and is also in Settings. Piano tuning is a choice (once a year recommended, twice a year, every three months). When you edit a clause, fill-in parts show as plain [labels] with your current answer next to each, plus a preview of how families will read it, instead of {{codes}}. No migration.
- Security fixes from the 18 Nov 2026 scan. Every page now tells browsers not to let other sites frame it (protects sign-in and two-step setup from clickjacking) and sends the standard nosniff, referrer and HTTPS-only headers. The two-step setup QR code is shown as an image instead of being inserted as HTML. The daily and 15-minute background jobs check their secret in constant time. Two old invite endpoints that accepted a user id from the browser now refuse on the live site (they were already blocked by the MFA check). `.env` files can no longer be committed by accident. No migration. Triage of all 136 findings: `docs/security/scan-2026-11-18.md`.
- Revoke and restore access. In the admin console, Team and Studios now have Revoke access (press twice to confirm) and Restore access for each staff member and studio owner. Revoking blocks their sign-in and every action straight away; they show as Access revoked. On a student profile in a real studio, the studio owner or an admin can revoke one parent's access to that studio's family app (other studios they belong to are untouched) and restore it later. All changes are logged. Needs migration 0020_revoke_access (apply before merging).
- Sign-in status and "Send sign-in link" for everyone Keystone invites. In the admin console, Team shows each staff member as Active (with their last sign-in) or Not signed in yet, and Studios shows the same for each studio owner. Each one has a Send sign-in link button that emails a fresh one-time link. Owners and staff who have never signed in also get the authenticator app buttons. On a student profile in a real studio, each parent shows Active, Not signed in yet or Enrolment form not finished, with the same button. A sign-in link works once and expires in about an hour, so nobody has to go into Supabase to resend an invite. Sends are logged (platform audit or studio auth audit) and limited to one per person per minute. No migration.
- Fixed: signing in with Microsoft or Google could finish on a "This sign-in link has expired" page even though sign-in had worked. The page now goes straight on to two-step sign-in and the app. If a Microsoft or Google sign-in really does fail, the message no longer talks about an email link. No migration.
